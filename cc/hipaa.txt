HIPAA and HITECH Compliance for Health Care

Subtitle:
Managing Storage Systems and Unstructured Health Information for Regulatory Compliance

The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act and the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act establish rules and regulations for the health care industry. Likewise delivers software solutions that help the health care industry comply with HIPAA, HITECH, and other regulations. The software secures, monitors, and audits protected heath information stored on file servers and NAS systems to guarantee the data's confidentiality, integrity, and availability. 

*Comply with HIPAA, HITECH, and other health care regulations.

*Mitigate the risk of noncompliance, security breeches, and privacy violations. 

*Protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of unstructured health care information.

*Reduce the costs of compliance, storage, information life cycle management, and security.
